01 Tournament overview

  • The championship is organised by the Computer Science Engineering department of Quli Qutub Shah Government Polytechnic (QQSGP).
  • The game is Free Fire. Squad size for this tournament is 2 to 4 players.
  • Registration is completed on the tournament website. Entries are stored by the organizing team and reviewed before matches.
  • The event is played as [Organizer: enter match format — online or on campus] with matches on [Organizer: enter match date].
  • Tournament schedule, bracket size and the number of accepted squads: [Organizer: enter schedule and team limit].

02 Eligibility and college participation

  • Squads register under the college department they represent. The department list on the registration form is the only accepted list: CSE, EEE, Mechanical Engineering, Printing Technology, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Civil Engineering.
  • Every player must be an enrolled student of QQSGP unless the organizers announce otherwise: [Organizer: confirm whether students from other colleges may take part].
  • Age limits and ID checks: [Organizer: enter age limit and ID policy].
  • Registration fee, if any: [Organizer: enter entry fee or state that it is free]. No fee is collected anywhere on this website.
  • An organizer may ask for college ID proof at any point. False information may lead to disqualification under rule 14.

03 Team formation

  • A team registers with two, three or four players. There is no five-player requirement in this tournament.
  • The registration form always asks for two mandatory players — the leader and Player 2. Player 3 and Player 4 are optional: add their names only if they are playing.
  • The first player entered is treated as the team leader and is the primary contact for the organizers.
  • Player names and WhatsApp numbers must belong to the actual players. Submitting a friend's number for the leader is not allowed.
  • A player may appear in only one squad. Duplicate players across squads may be removed by the organizers.
  • Team name must be unique for the tournament and must not contain offensive, casteist, communal or political language.
  • Substitutions: [Organizer: state whether substitutes are allowed and until when]. Until the organizers publish a substitution policy, the roster submitted on the form stands as the playing roster.

04 Player registration and in-game names

  • Register only through the tournament website. One team = one submission. Submitting the same team name twice on the same day is rejected by the server.
  • WhatsApp numbers are required for the leader and Player 2 only, and must be 10-digit mobile numbers. Both numbers cannot be the same.
  • Player names must match the in-game names players actually use in the room.
  • Free Fire UID collection: [Organizer: confirm whether UIDs will be collected before the match, and how]. The registration form currently records player names and contact numbers, not UIDs.
  • Keep the leader's WhatsApp active. Room details and match updates are sent to that number.

05 Registration approval and waitlist

  • Every submission is recorded with one of four statuses, visible to the organizers: PENDING (received, not yet reviewed), APPROVED (confirmed slot), WAITLISTED (held, may be promoted if a slot frees up) and REJECTED (not accepted).
  • A team is not confirmed until an organizer marks it APPROVED. Approval is communicated on the leader's WhatsApp number.
  • Rejected and waitlisted entries are kept as a record; they are not deleted from the tournament sheet.
  • The number of slots and the basis for selection: [Organizer: enter total slots and selection basis].
  • Incomplete or incorrect details may be sent back for correction instead of being approved.

10 Fair play and prohibited behaviour

  • The following are prohibited and lead to immediate disqualification:
    • Hacks, mods, scripts, aim assist, wall hacks, auto-headshot tools or any third-party game modification.
    • Emulators, tablets, controllers or other devices unless the organizers have announced support for them: [Organizer: state whether emulators or external devices are allowed].
    • Teaming with opposing squads, deliberate feeding, or match fixing.
    • Abusing exploits or bugs to gain an advantage.
    • Account sharing, or playing from an account other than the registered one.
    • Abusive, communal, casteist or disrespectful behaviour towards players or organizers, in chat or on stream.
  • Screenshots or recordings may be requested as evidence for any of the above.
  • Any player found in violation may be removed with their squad.

11 Disconnections and technical issues

  • Individual disconnections during a match are not grounds for a rematch. The squad continues with the players still in the room.
  • Room crashes, server outages or a lobby that fails to start the match: [Organizer: state the remake policy].
  • Report an issue immediately on the leader's registered WhatsApp number. Reports after the match ends may not be entertained.
  • Players are responsible for their own device, network and battery.

12 Late arrival and no-show policy

  • Grace period after reporting time: [Organizer: enter grace period].
  • A squad that has not joined the room when the match starts may be marked as a no-show for that match.
  • Repeated no-shows by an approved squad may free its slot for a waitlisted team under rule 5.
  • No-show does not delete the registration record.

13 Disputes, screenshots and evidence

  • Raise a dispute on the leader's registered WhatsApp number within [Organizer: enter dispute window, e.g. 15 minutes] of the match ending.
  • Disputes must include evidence: a screenshot or screen recording showing the result, the scoreboard, and the issue itself.
  • Edited or cropped screenshots may be rejected. Full-screen originals are preferred.
  • The organizers' decision after reviewing the evidence is final.

14 Disqualification

  • Disqualification is recorded against the team's registration entry. The entry is not deleted.
  • A disqualified squad may be replaced by a waitlisted squad at the organizers' discretion.
