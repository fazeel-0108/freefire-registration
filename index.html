THE TOURNAMENT

Built for campus squads, run like a real event.

The QQSGP Free Fire Championship is organised by the Computer Science Engineering department of Quli Qutub Shah Government Polytechnic. Squads from every department register here, the organizers review each entry, and room details reach the team leader on WhatsApp.

  • 2, 3 or 4 players — a short-handed squad is a valid squad.
  • One clean entry per team — every submission lands in the tournament sheet.
  • Reviewed, not automatic — a slot is confirmed only after the organizers approve it.
